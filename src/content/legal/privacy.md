This Privacy Policy explains how {{entity.name}} ("Globalink", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal data when you use Estori at estori.app (the "Service"). Globalink is the controller of the personal data described here.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, display name, optional username, avatar and bio, a one-way hash of your password, and, if you sign in with another provider, that provider's account identifier.

**Sign-in and security information.** Your IP address, browser and device details, active sessions, sign-in attempts and security events such as password changes. We use these to keep accounts secure and to prevent abuse.

**Your content.** Prompts and chat messages, images you upload, the files of the sites built for you and their history, product page text, comments, screenshots of your sites, and the stars, favorites and likes you give.

**Marketplace information.** Purchases you make or receive, their amounts and dates, and, for sellers, the identifier of your connected Stripe account and whether it can receive payouts. Card numbers and bank details go directly to Stripe. We never receive or store them.

**Early access requests.** Which upcoming programs you asked to join, and when.

**Site views.** When someone views a public site, we store a one-way hash so each viewer is counted once. We don't store the viewer's IP address with it.

**Usage information.** On estori.app we collect the pages you view, without the part of the address after a question mark, where you click, your device and browser, your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it (such as country, region and city), errors the Service runs into, and recordings of some sessions. We also note what you do in Estori, such as building and publishing sites, your plan and subscription, credit purchases, and sales in your stores. We record layouts and clicks, not what's written on the page or what you type. Recordings show no images. They skip your orders, shopper emails, profile and payments setup, settings and billing, and admin pages, and never show the preview of your store. We link this to your account by its id, never by your email address or name. Section 4 says how to turn it off.

**Page view counts.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views on Estori's pages, previews and stores served on estori.app addresses, with the page, the referring site, and the visitor's browser, device and country. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify visitors.

We don't collect sensitive personal data on purpose. Please don't include it in prompts or sites unless your store needs it.

## 2. How we use it

We use personal data to:

- provide the Service: build, edit, preview, host and publish your sites, and run Discover and the marketplace;
- process purchases, payouts, refunds and chargebacks;
- secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our [Terms of Service](/terms);
- answer your requests and send you messages about your account and the Service;
- fix problems and improve the Service;
- meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use or share it for targeted advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as described here.

**Service providers** that process it for us under contract:

- **Cloudflare**: hosting, databases, file storage, AI request routing, browser rendering for site screenshots and for pages you ask the AI to look at, sending account emails such as password resets and email confirmations, sending the order emails stores send their shoppers, operational logs, and page view counts through Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
- **PostHog** (hosted in the United States): product analytics, session recordings and error reports for estori.app, and the switches that turn parts of the Service on or pause them. PostHog receives the usage information in section 1, including your IP address, linked to your account's id. We don't send it your email address or name.
- **AI model providers** (currently Anthropic and Google): your prompts, messages, images and site files are sent to the model you choose, or to a fallback model when the chosen one is unavailable, to generate and edit your site.
- **Stripe**: payments, seller onboarding and payouts. Stripe receives buyers', shoppers' and sellers' email addresses, shoppers' shipping addresses, and the payment and payout details people give Stripe directly.
- **Fulfillment partners**: when a store sells products from the Estori catalog, the shopper's name, shipping address, email and phone number go to the partner that ships the order.
- **GitHub**: only if you choose to export a site to GitHub.

**Other users and the public.** Public sites, their screenshots, product pages, your display name and avatar, and public stats are visible to everyone. A paid site's seller sees that a sale happened; its buyers' details aren't shown to them. A store's owner sees the orders their store takes, with each shopper's name, email, phone number (when given) and shipping address.

**Legal and safety reasons.** When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or Globalink.

**Business transfers.** If Globalink is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of it, subject to this Policy.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

We use the cookies the Service needs to work:

- a sign-in session cookie;
- a security cookie that protects forms against cross-site requests;
- short-lived cookies used while you sign in with another provider;
- short-lived cookies that let you preview your own sites.

Your browser also saves a few preferences (theme, sidebar state and sort order) and a temporary cache of pages you've viewed, to make the Service faster. We use PostHog's analytics cookie and browser storage on estori.app to measure how the Service is used, as section 1 describes. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies. We don't use advertising cookies. To turn analytics off, use the Analytics setting in Settings, or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser. Stores built with Estori don't load PostHog.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep account information and your content while your account exists. When you delete a site, it's removed from your account and from Discover right away, but its stored files, conversations and screenshots may remain in our systems until they're erased. To have your account and all personal data about you erased, email {{contact.privacy}}. We complete erasure within 45 days, except where we must keep information longer, for example purchase records for tax and accounting, or information needed to resolve disputes and prevent abuse. Copies in backups are deleted on their normal cycle. We keep security records, such as sign-in history, to protect accounts and the Service, and delete them when we erase your account.

## 6. Your rights and choices

You can update your account information in Settings.

You can turn analytics off at any time with the Analytics setting in Settings. We also treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to turn analytics off, and keep it off for your account. With analytics off, we still count the AI credits your builds use, without anything that identifies you, to manage what the Service costs to run. We also still ask PostHog which switches apply to your account, using its id and your plan.

You can also ask us to:

- confirm whether we process your personal data and give you access to it;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data;
- give you a copy of your data in a portable format;
- stop processing your data for a particular purpose, where the law gives you that right.

To make a request, email {{contact.privacy}} from the address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity. We respond within 45 days, and we may extend that once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, telling you why. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the subject "Appeal". We'll respond to an appeal within 60 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general or your data protection authority.

**Residents of Texas, California and other US states** with privacy laws have the rights above as their state's law provides. We don't sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising, and we don't process sensitive data in ways that require consent.

**People in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland** also have the right to object to processing and to restrict it, and to complain to a data protection authority. We rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (to provide the Service and the marketplace), our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse and improving the Service, balanced against your rights), and legal obligations (tax and accounting records).

## 7. International transfers

We're based in the United States and process personal data in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. When we transfer personal data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in our providers' terms.

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with measures such as encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children

The Service is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us personal data, contact {{contact.privacy}} and we'll delete it.

## 10. Your store's visitors

When people visit a store you build, the store may collect their information, for example orders or contact forms. We store and process that information on your behalf to run your store. You're responsible for telling your visitors how your store uses their data and for any privacy notice your store needs.

Shoppers who pay at a store give their name, email, shipping address and card details to Stripe at checkout. We receive the name, email, phone number (when given) and shipping address to record the order, deliver catalog products and show the order to the store's owner, who sells to them. We use the shopper's email to send the store's order emails through Estori: an order confirmation, shipping updates and refund notices. They come from the store's name, sent by Estori, and replies go to the store. We keep a record of each email, including the address it went to, for 90 days.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. If a change is material, we'll tell you through the Service or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this Policy last changed.
