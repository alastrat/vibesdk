This policy covers purchases of paid sites, and payments for Pro, Premium and extra credits, on Estori, sold by {{entity.name}} ("Globalink", "we") as merchant of record. Building on the free plan costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund for it. Purchases from a store built on Estori are sold by that store's owner, and follow that store's own refund policy.

## When you can get a refund

Because a paid site is delivered as soon as you buy it, and you keep the files once you start a site from it, sales are final, with two exceptions. You can get a full refund if:

- **the site can't be used**: after your purchase, "Use this site" fails or the copied site doesn't load, and we can't fix it within a reasonable time; or
- **you were charged twice** for the same site.

## How to ask

Email {{contact.support}} within 14 days of the purchase. Include the name of the site, plan or extra credits, the email address you paid with, and what went wrong. We'll reply within 5 business days, and we may try to fix the problem before refunding.

## What happens when we refund

- The money goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
- The seller's share of the sale is reversed.
- We may remove your access to the refunded site, including copies made from it.

## Pro, Premium and extra credits

Payments for Pro, Premium and extra credits are refundable only when you were charged twice, or when your account couldn't use Estori. Ask within 14 days of the payment, as described under "How to ask".

A refund of extra credits removes the unused credits it covers.

Cancelling a plan stops its renewal; the current period isn't refunded.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of contacting us, we may suspend your access to the site while the dispute is open. Please contact us first; we can usually resolve problems faster.

## Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the law where you live.
