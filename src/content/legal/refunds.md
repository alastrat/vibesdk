This policy covers purchases of paid sites on Estori, sold by {{entity.name}} ("Globalink", "we") as merchant of record. Building on Estori is free, so there's nothing to refund for building.

## When you can get a refund

Because a paid site is delivered as soon as you buy it, and you keep the files once you start a site from it, sales are final, with two exceptions. You can get a full refund if:

- **the site can't be used**: after your purchase, "Use this site" fails or the copied site doesn't load, and we can't fix it within a reasonable time; or
- **you were charged twice** for the same site.

## How to ask

Email {{contact.support}} within 14 days of the purchase. Include the name of the site, the email address you bought it with, and what went wrong. We'll reply within 5 business days, and we may try to fix the problem before refunding.

## What happens when we refund

- The money goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
- The seller's share of the sale is reversed.
- We may remove your access to the refunded site, including copies made from it.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of contacting us, we may suspend your access to the site while the dispute is open. Please contact us first; we can usually resolve problems faster.

## Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the law where you live.
